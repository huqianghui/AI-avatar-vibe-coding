<objective>
Refactor the scoring architecture from "CU content + voice with LLM/mock fallbacks" to "LLM content + CU voice only" with no mock fallback.

Purpose: CU cannot evaluate content accurately (no external context, no role understanding). The LLM engine already has a complete context-rich prompt. Mock fallback masks real failures by generating fake passing scores.

Output: Clean scoring separation — LLM handles 5 content dimensions, CU handles 4 voice dimensions, failures return 503 instead of mock scores.
</objective>
