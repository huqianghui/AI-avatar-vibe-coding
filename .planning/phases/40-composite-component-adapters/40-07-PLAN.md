Purpose: DropdownMenu is deliberately run in its OWN dedicated plan/session, separate from Select (40-06), per research's explicit risk-isolation finding. It is sequenced LAST among the code-changing composites (before the verification-only ScrollArea plan) since it is the most structurally complex (15 exports, submenu nesting, dict-keyed checked state) and benefits from every pattern already proven in this phase (asChild shim from 40-01, coverage-backfill sequencing from 40-04/40-06).
