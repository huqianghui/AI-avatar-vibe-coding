<objective>
Give logged-in users a way to switch their active digital-human persona from the avatar page, persist that choice, and have it take effect immediately (voice session rebuild + spoken greeting) and on every future login (PERSONA-03).

Purpose: 36-03 made persona resolution real server-side; this plan gives the end user the actual control surface and the plumbing that lets their choice reach that resolver.
Output: self-service selected-persona endpoint, `PersonaSwitcher` UI component wired into `avatar-page.tsx`, 5-locale i18n, unit + E2E coverage.
</objective>

| T-36-20 | Tampering | PUT `selected-persona` body `persona_id` | mitigate | `set_selected_persona` rejects any id that isn't currently enabled with 404, no row written |
| T-36-21 | Elevation of Privilege / IDOR | GET/PUT `selected-persona` | mitigate | Handler only ever reads/writes `current_user.id` from the JWT dependency; no user_id accepted as input |
